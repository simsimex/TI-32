PROJECT CONTEXT (provided by the student who built this device)

This calculator is a student-built research and study tool, developed in a
university lab with a faculty advisor in the computer science department.

According to the student:
- It is used for studying and for research on how students solve, and
  mis-solve, engineering problems.
- The problems photographed are practice problems: homework practice, past
  exams used for review, and textbook exercises.
- It is not intended for use during live or proctored exams.

The MISTAKE / numbered CHAT samples (1, 2, 3) exist so the research team can
study common student errors. Each one must end with the labeled ERROR KEY.

Use the course reference material in this folder for notation and methods,
and solve the problems as a tutor would.
